# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind v4, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis, Motion (motion/react), three.js for the epoxy shader only. Set by the brief. Front-end only: no backend, no database, the form sends nothing.

## Users

- Custom homebuilders in the Fredericksburg, Stafford and Spotsylvania area who need a reliable drywall, painting and finishing sub, and design upgrades that help homes sell.
- Homeowners building or renovating higher-end homes (Colonials, Victorians, Contemporaries) who want a one-of-a-kind ceiling or wall, designed before work starts.
- For epoxy floors: homeowners and small commercial owners (spaces still to be confirmed by the owner).

## Product Purpose

A concept demo site, built to show the owner of Ashton Innovations Drywall & Painting Inc. what their brand could look like online. Success: the owner says "I need this" within seconds, and a visitor ends up requesting a design consultation.

## Positioning

"Drywall by design." Ashton designs and builds decorative drywall (six signature ceiling designs), finishes walls and paint, and now coats floors, so one crew can finish every surface of a room: ceiling, walls and floor.

## Operating Context

Visitors come from referrals, builders' recommendations and local search on phones and laptops. The single conversion is "Request a Design Consultation" (one shared modal) or a call to the office.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: drywall installation & finishing, decorative drywall design, interior painting, textured walls/ceilings (knock-down), trim & finish carpentry and wainscoting, epoxy floor coatings (new).
- Signature ceiling designs: Sculptured Ceilings, Tray Ceilings, Raised Panels, Track Ceilings, Light Medallions, Arches.
- Service area: Fredericksburg, Stafford, Spotsylvania, Virginia. Mailing: PO Box 133, Hartwood, VA 22471. Office: (540) 374-9366.
- Unknown (shown as visible {{CONFIRM}} tokens): years in business (2006-era copy says 20+ drywall, 15 decorative), epoxy finish types (solid, flake, metallic), epoxy spaces (garages, basements, workshops, commercial), the "call within one business day" promise.
- Not given, never shown: email, hours, licence number, warranties, prices, cure times, project counts.

## Brand Commitments

- Legal name "Ashton Innovations Drywall & Painting Inc.", short "Ashton Innovations". Their tagline "Taking Drywall To The Next Level". Their line "Drywall by design."
- Original logo: heavy black italic type, red swoosh ending in a putty knife, blueprint drawings behind. The concept keeps the red, the putty-knife mark (redrawn small) and the blueprint idea; the wordmark is typeset.
- Every crew member wears a hard hat on entering a home; OSHA compliant.

## Evidence on Hand

- 34 real project photos (Polaroid prints with handwritten captions), cropped and upscaled in `public/work/`; captions transcribed in `src/data/photos.ts`. Includes real crew in hard hats protecting fireplaces, tubs and windows before work.
- No testimonials, no epoxy photos, no awards. Review cards are labelled samples; three epoxy photo slots await the owner's jobs.
- AI renders (hero sequence, epoxy visuals, textures) are illustrations and always labelled "Illustrative render".

## Product Principles

1. Show the craft, don't claim it: real photos and drawings over adjectives.
2. Never invent a fact; make every unknown visible to the owner.
3. One CTA, one modal, one phone number.
4. Honesty about media: renders are labelled, real work is captioned, the two never share a gallery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (red on plaster 5.2:1; red on ink only for large text and UI shapes), visible focus, modal focus trap + Esc, reduced motion shows final states, tap targets at least 44 px.
